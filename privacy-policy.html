Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Welcome to Sophia Carter Health. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways when you visit our website:

  • Personal Data: We collect personally identifiable information that you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out our contact form, subscribe to a newsletter, or email us. This may include your name, email address, subject, and message content.
  • Usage Data: We automatically collect data when you navigate our site, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, time spent on pages, and click behavior.

2. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies (like web beacons and pixels) to analyze visitor traffic, remember preferences, and improve user experiences.

  • Cookies: Small data files stored on your device. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, some portions of our Service may not function properly.
  • Meta Pixel (formerly Facebook Pixel): We use the Meta Pixel on our website. The Meta Pixel is an analytics tool that allows us to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns, understand the actions people take on our site, and build targeted custom audiences for future advertisements. The data collected via the Meta Pixel is stored and processed by Meta (Facebook, Inc.) and is subject to their data policy.
  • Opt-Out: You can manage your Facebook ad settings and opt-out of interest-based ads via your Facebook account settings, or by visiting third-party opt-out portals like the Digital Advertising Alliance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various purposes, including to:

  • Operate, maintain, and optimize our website and user experience.
  • Respond to customer support requests, inquiries, and emails.
  • Analyze site performance and track visitor behavior (e.g. scroll depth and click events) using analytical scripts.
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions, monitor against theft, and protect against criminal activity.
  • Share relevant affiliate offers with your consent or track conversions through affiliate link clicks.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or serving our users (such as web hosting partners, analytical services, and email delivery systems), as long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

We may also release information when its release is appropriate to comply with the law, enforce our site policies, or protect ours or others' rights, property, or safety.

5. Security of Your Data

The security of your personal data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under data privacy laws (such as GDPR or CCPA) regarding the personal information we hold about you. These rights may include the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we have collected, or the right to opt-out of certain tracking cookies.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at support.wellnesswithsophia@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for use by children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to remove that information from our servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
