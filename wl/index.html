From Stuck to Strong: How This "Costa Rican Tea" Helped Me Feel Like Myself Again at 43

I Tried Everything to Feel Like Myself Again. Then a Simple Daily "Tea Recipe"
Helped Me Find My Consistency and Confidence.

By Diane – Mom, Working Woman, and Proud of the Habit That Stuck.

Hi, I'm Diane. I'm 43 years old, and I'm sharing my story because I know how discouraging it feels when your body just doesn't respond the way it used to, no matter how hard you try. This isn't medical advice — just my own personal experience. After my kids grew older and life got busier with work, my daily habits shifted without me even realizing it. Between deadlines, errands, and constant stress, I wasn't eating the way I used to — and over the years, I noticed my body changing along with it.

I tried watching my portions. I tried cutting back on late-night snacking. I tried squeezing in workouts when I could. But staying consistent was the hard part. At a routine doctor's visit, my physician mentioned that a few of my numbers weren't where they should be. It wasn't a diagnosis. Just a gentle nudge to start paying closer attention. That conversation stuck with me longer than I expected. Like a lot of people, stressful stretches made it nearly impossible to keep up healthy habits, and more often than not, I found comfort in food.
