It's Shockingly Simple,
This 7-Second Morning Routine Transformed My Life and My Body!

I'm down 90 lbs and still eating all of my favorite foods!

By Suzie D. 49 from Fort Worth, TX.

For many years, there's been a small part of me that has resented my 3 kids for helping to do their part in the destruction of my waistline.

I know that this makes me sound completely awful, and I feel terrible about it, but I have to tell you that I absolutely love and adore my kids more than anything in the entire universe....but the flab that had stuck around my middle section remained there for almost THREE FULL DECADES after my youngest child was born!

I had spent countless hours at the gym, despite absolutely hating every minute of it! I cut out all of the foods I enjoyed and I tried every weight-loss program imaginable, yet I still couldn't shed the extra pounds.

Adding to the stress of work, family responsibilities, and striving to be the perfect wife and mom didn't help either. Each morning, I felt more down and defeated when I looked in the mirror, hoping to see a fit, trim, and transformed version of myself....instead, I only saw the same unhealthy, exhausted version of me!

It's no surprise that intimacy with my husband had completely vanished, and I'd caught him staring at younger, more energetic women, despite everything I did for him and our family each and every day!

I mistakenly tried to be perfect for everyone, but when I looked in the mirror, I saw nothing that was perfect about myself!

I was always exhausted, angry, and frumpy. And when my doctor told me that my health was at a VERY critical point, I realized that something needed to change. I couldn't continue being THAT person!

I needed a solution that fit my very hectic schedule. Since I couldn't magically add more hours to my day, I was determined to search the internet for something faster and easier. There just had to be something that would work for me!

Then, one day, I had an epiphany when my personal doctor handed me a note. On this note were a few simple phrases: "Suzie, please try this simple, '7-Second Morning Routine' every single morning.....keep in mind that your health is declining, and without immediate changes, you're at risk for a heart attack, diabetes, or even bariatric surgery." Following this note, there was a web link to a video that he suggested that I watch immediately.

I decided to take that leap....and I thank God that I did....I only wish I had found this morning routine sooner!

It turns out that my issues had absolutely NOTHING to do with my diet, my family's genetics, or even the amount of exercise I could do. Many people praise fasting and some even swear by the intermittent fasting craze, but I don't have to worry about getting involved with any of that at all, not with this simple, "7-Second Morning Routine."

Now, after almost 3 decades of being stuck at 215 pounds, I'm proudly back down to 125 pounds and I couldn't be happier! At 49, my vitals all look good, my bloodwork is completely within all of the normal ranges, and I'm now in the best shape of my life.

I've even shared this simple, "7-Second Morning Routine" with my dear niece, a proud mom of 4; she bounced back from her last pregnancy in less than 8 months using the same simple, "7-Second Morning Routine!"

This simple, "7-Second Morning Routine" works so well, I feel completely energized, I'm sleeping better at night, my dark, under-eye circles have vanished, my skin is clearer than it's been in the longest time, and even my hair has grown in thicker, stronger, and shinier than it has been in a very long time! 😳

And because I'm so happy both seeing and feeling all of these fantastic results, my dear husband Matthew is now hooked on this simple, "7-Second Morning Routine" too. I have to admit, my husband looks better than ever! I no longer worry that another woman has caught his eye, as he tells me that my renewed confidence and revitalized energy has his heart beating as nervously as it did when he first proposed to me! I have to admit, having him drop everything just to come up to me and wrap his loving arms around me when I'm simply standing at the kitchen sink, washing dishes, it makes me feel like I'm 18 again, and any intimacy issues we had are no longer issues either!

The best part – My cravings are all but gone, but if I really want to, I'm still able to eat all of my favorite foods and treats- I don't ever need to sacrifice taste for anything!

Words can't begin to describe how happy we all are! Life has changed in so many positive ways for all of us too!

This simple, "7-Second Morning Routine" was 10x more effective for me than exercising, cutting calories, and even intermittent fasting ever were....

But you should go ahead and give it a try, experience it for yourself. Don't sit on the sidelines of your life! Don't let your life just pass you by, like I did, and for FAR TOO LONG!
